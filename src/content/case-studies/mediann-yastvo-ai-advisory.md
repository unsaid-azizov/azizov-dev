## The brief

Mediann.dev was building Yastvo AI, a product for calorie and macros tracking. The product needed a nutritionist chat so users could get recommendations and resolve specific questions inside the experience.

## Our role

We joined as the AI/ML specialist: reviewed the product material, collected requirements, and designed a multi-agent system around the team's actual use case rather than adding a generic chat interface.
