## The problem

Inbound calls were tied to human availability. When the team was unavailable, the business risked missing the context and urgency behind a valuable enquiry.

## What we built

An AI receptionist that answers inbound calls, captures the information required to qualify a lead, and carries each interaction into the next operational step.

## Reported outcome

Ekron reported that the system helped capture around £10K in additional work. The same agent captured a £1,200 lead while the owner was away from the office, which the client later converted. For a home-service team, that is the same moment a technician is on a roof, at a job, or driving between appointments.
