## The problem

The sales team received demand across multiple channels, which made it difficult to qualify enquiries consistently or preserve the context needed to sell effectively.

## What we built

An AI-led qualification workflow that structures new enquiries, makes product and pricing information available, and sends a complete record to Bitrix24.
