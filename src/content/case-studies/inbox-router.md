## The problem

Messages arrived across 16 departmental inboxes. Ownership was hard to track, demand competed with operational email, and every handoff relied on manual sorting.

## What we built

An AI router that reads incoming messages, classifies them by purpose and department, batches the right messages together, and creates structured CRM handoffs for the team responsible.

## Reported outcome

The operating model now connects 16 inboxes to one routing layer. Teams receive grouped, contextual work in the CRM instead of needing to triage the email estate manually.
