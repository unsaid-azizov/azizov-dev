## What the demo shows

The WhatsApp agent receives a property enquiry, guides the conversation toward the details needed for the next step, and books the viewing into Google Calendar without making the lead wait for a manual response.

## Why this matters

For real-estate brokers, the first reply often determines whether an interested buyer books a viewing or disappears. The workflow gives the prospect a clear next step, creates the appointment in Google Calendar, and preserves the property and lead context the team needs to follow up properly.
