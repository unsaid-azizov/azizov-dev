## What the system handles

It answers the calls your team cannot get to, asks the questions that determine whether a job is worth pursuing, and routes the result into the workflow your office already uses.

## What changes

Your team stops returning to a pile of missed calls with no context. Qualified opportunities arrive with a clear reason for the call, the right contact details, and the next action already assigned.
