## What the system handles

It makes approved company knowledge searchable in plain language, with access controls and links back to the underlying source material. A technician, operator, or sales teammate can find the relevant process without hunting through folders or asking the same question again.

## What changes

The business stops treating knowledge as a pile of PDFs, chats, and tribal memory. People get faster, more consistent answers while the source documents remain the authority.

## Built for operational reality

A useful RAG assistant needs more than document upload. We define sources, permissions, retrieval quality, update ownership, and when the assistant should say it does not know. That is what makes it safe enough to use at work.
