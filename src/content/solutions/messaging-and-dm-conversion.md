## What the system handles

It turns inbound messages and social conversations into structured qualification instead of leaving your team to repeat the same answers and manually copy context between apps.

## What changes

For a coach, a comment can become a qualified DM. For a home-service team, a message can become a scoped enquiry and booked job. The channel changes; the commercial outcome does not.
