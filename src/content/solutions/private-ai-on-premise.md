## What the system handles

We assess the workflow, data sensitivity, access model, and deployment constraints before selecting an architecture. Depending on the requirements, that can mean a local model, a private cloud environment, a tightly controlled API layer, or a hybrid setup.

## What changes

Your team gets an AI system designed around security, permissions, auditability, and operational use—not a consumer chatbot pasted into a sensitive workflow.

## Important distinction

“On-premise” is not automatically the right answer. The right design depends on the data, compliance requirements, reliability needs, and the cost of maintaining the system. We map those trade-offs before recommending a build.
